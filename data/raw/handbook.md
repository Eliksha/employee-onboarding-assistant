# NovaTech Solutions Employee Handbook (Synthetic - fictional company)

NovaTech Solutions is a fictional IT services company with about 500 employees across Bengaluru, Pune and Hyderabad. This handbook is synthetic sample data for training purposes only.

## 1. Working Hours and Attendance
Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday. Core hours, when all employees should be available, are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
NovaTech follows a hybrid model: employees work from the office on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday, and may work from home on Monday and Friday with manager approval for exceptions.
Attendance is marked on the NovaConnect portal. Late arrival of more than 30 minutes, more than 3 times in a month, is flagged to the manager.

## 2. Leave Policy
### 2.1 Casual Leave
Employees get 8 casual leaves per calendar year. For employees who join during the year, casual leave is prorated at 0.67 days per month of service. Casual leave can be taken for a maximum of 3 consecutive days. Apply on NovaConnect at least 2 days in advance except in emergencies.
### 2.2 Sick Leave
Employees get 8 sick leaves per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days.
### 2.3 Earned Leave
Earned leave accrues at 1.5 days per month after completing the 6-month probation period, for a total of 18 days per year. Earned leave can be carried forward up to 30 days. Employees on probation cannot avail earned leave.
### 2.4 Maternity, Paternity and Bereavement Leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks. Paternity leave is 10 working days and must be taken within 3 months of the child's birth. Bereavement leave is 3 days for the loss of an immediate family member.
### 2.5 Public Holidays
NovaTech observes 12 public holidays and 2 floating holidays per year. The holiday list is published on NovaConnect in January.

## 3. Payroll
Salary is credited on the last working day of every month. Payslips are available on NovaConnect by the 3rd of the next month.
New joiners must submit a provisional tax declaration within the first 2 weeks of joining. Proof of investments must be submitted by 31 January each year.
For payroll queries, contact payroll@novatech.example.

## 4. Benefits
### 4.1 Health Insurance
Group health insurance with a cover of Rs 5 lakh is effective from the first day of employment. It covers the employee, spouse and up to 2 children. New joiners must enrol within 15 days of joining on NovaConnect.
### 4.2 Provident Fund
Both the employee and NovaTech contribute 12 percent of basic salary to the Provident Fund.
### 4.3 Learning Budget
Employees who have completed probation get a learning budget of Rs 30,000 per year for courses and certifications, subject to manager approval.
### 4.4 Wellness
Employees can claim up to Rs 6,000 per year for gym or wellness expenses. A 24x7 Employee Assistance Programme helpline offers free confidential counselling.

## 5. IT Setup and Support
### 5.1 Laptop and Accounts
IT issues a laptop and ID card on the first day of joining. Company email and accounts are created before the joining date.
### 5.2 Multi-Factor Authentication
All employees must set up multi-factor authentication (MFA) using the Microsoft Authenticator app within 24 hours of receiving their credentials.
### 5.3 VPN
To work remotely, install the NovaVPN client from the ServiceHub portal and sign in with your company email and MFA code.
### 5.4 Software Requests
Request additional software through the ServiceHub portal. Requests need manager approval and are completed within 3 working days.
### 5.5 Lost or Stolen Devices
Report a lost or stolen laptop to it-help@novatech.example within 2 hours of discovering the loss.
### 5.6 Passwords
Passwords must be at least 12 characters and are changed every 90 days. Use the self-service reset page if you forget your password.

## 6. Code of Conduct and POSH
Employees must treat colleagues, clients and partners with respect and avoid conflicts of interest. NovaTech has a zero-tolerance policy for harassment. Complaints under the Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) policy can be made to the Internal Committee. Concerns can also be raised confidentially at ethics@novatech.example or through the anonymous ethics hotline.

## 7. Expenses and Travel
Business expense claims must be submitted on NovaConnect within 30 days of the expense, with receipts for any amount above Rs 500. Claims need manager approval. Claims submitted by the 20th of a month are reimbursed with that month's payroll. A company cab is provided for employees working beyond 9:00 PM.

## 8. Performance Management
The performance cycle runs from April to March, with a mid-year review in October and an annual review in March. New joiners set 30-60-90 day goals with their manager. The probation period is 6 months, followed by a confirmation review.

## 9. Resignation and Exit
The notice period is 60 days after confirmation and 30 days during probation. An exit interview is held in the last week. Full and final settlement is completed within 45 days of the last working day.
