# NovaTech Solutions Onboarding FAQs (Synthetic)

**Q:** How many casual leaves do I get?
**A:** 8 per calendar year; prorated at 0.67 days per month if you join mid-year (Handbook 2.1).

**Q:** What is the maximum casual leave I can take at once?
**A:** 3 consecutive days (Handbook 2.1).

**Q:** How many sick leaves are there?
**A:** 8 per year. A medical certificate is needed for more than 2 consecutive days (Handbook 2.2).

**Q:** When can I use earned leave?
**A:** After completing the 6-month probation; it accrues at 1.5 days per month (Handbook 2.3).

**Q:** How long is maternity leave?
**A:** 26 weeks (Handbook 2.4).

**Q:** How long is paternity leave?
**A:** 10 working days, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth (Handbook 2.4).

**Q:** How many public holidays are there?
**A:** 12 public holidays and 2 floating holidays per year (Handbook 2.5).

**Q:** What are the office days under the hybrid policy?
**A:** Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday in the office (Handbook 1).

**Q:** When is salary credited?
**A:** On the last working day of every month (Handbook 3).

**Q:** Where do I find my payslip?
**A:** On NovaConnect by the 3rd of the next month (Handbook 3).

**Q:** When must I submit my tax declaration?
**A:** A provisional declaration within 2 weeks of joining; proofs by 31 January (Handbook 3).

**Q:** When does my health insurance start?
**A:** From the first day; enrol within 15 days on NovaConnect (Handbook 4.1).

**Q:** Who is covered under health insurance?
**A:** Employee, spouse and up to 2 children, with Rs 5 lakh cover (Handbook 4.1).

**Q:** What is the learning budget?
**A:** Rs 30,000 per year after probation, with manager approval (Handbook 4.3).

**Q:** Is there a wellness benefit?
**A:** Up to Rs 6,000 per year for gym/wellness plus a 24x7 counselling helpline (Handbook 4.4).

**Q:** When do I get my laptop?
**A:** On your first day of joining (Handbook 5.1).

**Q:** How do I set up MFA?
**A:** Use the Microsoft Authenticator app within 24 hours of getting credentials (Handbook 5.2).

**Q:** How do I connect to the VPN?
**A:** Install NovaVPN from ServiceHub and sign in with email and MFA code (Handbook 5.3).

**Q:** How do I request new software?
**A:** Raise a request on ServiceHub; manager approval is needed and it takes 3 working days (Handbook 5.4).

**Q:** What do I do if my laptop is lost?
**A:** Report to it-help@novatech.example within 2 hours (Handbook 5.5).

**Q:** How long is the probation period?
**A:** 6 months, followed by a confirmation review (Handbook 8).

**Q:** How do I claim expenses?
**A:** Submit on NovaConnect within 30 days; receipts for amounts above Rs 500 (Handbook 7).

**Q:** When is the performance review?
**A:** Mid-year in October and annual in March (Handbook 8).

**Q:** What is the notice period?
**A:** 60 days after confirmation; 30 days during probation (Handbook 9).

**Q:** Who do I contact to report harassment?
**A:** The POSH Internal Committee or ethics@novatech.example (Handbook 6).
